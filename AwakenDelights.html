Our Services

Artisanal Breads

Sourdough, multigrain, ciabatta, focaccia—baked daily from scratch using natural starters.

Pastries & Sweet Treats

Croissants, danishes, muffins, tarts, cookies—featuring seasonal flavors and unique twists.

Custom Cakes & Celebrations

Tiered cakes, cupcakes, cake pops, groom’s cakes—fully custom designs for birthdays, weddings, showers.

Catering & Corporate Orders

Breakfast boxes, pastry platters, dessert tables for offices, conferences, and events.

Baking Workshops & Classes

Small-group lessons on sourdough, pastry lamination, cake decorating—hands-on and take-home recipes.

Our Team Roles

Project Manager

  • Set realistic expectations and deadlines
  • Facilitate communication among team members
  • Resolve disputes and maintain morale
  • Solve problems as they arise
  • Ensure work and budget stay on track

Strategist

  • Draft the mission statement for the website
  • Research needs and expectations of intended users
  • Set short-term and long-term project goals

Content Specialist

  • Conduct research and analysis for site content
  • Organize, write, and edit web copy
  • Choose appropriate text, images, and multimedia to meet goals

Usability Expert

  • Test the site throughout development with intended users
  • Evaluate usability against similar sites
  • Ensure cultural appropriateness and accessibility standards

Designer

  • Create UI sketches and wireframes
  • Develop high-fidelity page mockups
  • Design graphic elements, color schemes, and typography

Developer

  • Program the front-end UI (HTML, CSS, JavaScript)
  • Implement back-end functionality (server-side code, databases)
  • Test across browsers and devices; revise as needed

Marketer

  • Locate and reach out to potential customers
  • Create and distribute content via blogs, mailings, social media
  • Develop special offers and campaigns to drive conversions
