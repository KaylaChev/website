Design Considerations

Overview of UX & technical principles applied at SweetCrust Bakery

Search Engine Optimization

On our Home page, we crafted a concise <title> (“SweetCrust Bakery | Fresh Artisan Bread & Pastries”) and meta‑description (“Discover our daily‑baked sourdough, croissants, and more, baked fresh in Hudson Valley”). We use semantic headings (<h1> on the page title, <h2> on sections), and include an XML sitemap at /sitemap.xml.

  • Blog: Unique meta tags for each recipe post; keyword‑rich URLs (e.g. /recipes/sourdough-starter).
  • Product pages: Structured data (JSON‑LD) for “Product” so Google shows pricing & ratings in search results.

User Interface Design Patterns

We leverage the Card Pattern on our Menu page to showcase each pastry: image at top, product name, short description, price, and “Add to Cart” button. This grouping makes it easy to scan and compare items.

Positional Awareness

Every subpage includes a breadcrumb trail at the top—e.g. Home > Menu > Croissants—so users always know where they are in our site hierarchy.

Effective Navigation

We implemented a sticky top navigation bar with clear links: Home, Menu, Order Online, About Us, Blog, Contact. On mobile, this condenses to a hamburger menu that slides out.

See our mobile nav CSS: nav.css#L45

Site Accessibility

  • All images include descriptive alt attributes (e.g. alt="Fully‑baked sourdough loaf").
  • Color contrast ratios exceed 4.5:1 for body text against backgrounds.
  • ARIA roles on custom components, e.g. role="navigation" on the hamburger menu.
  • Keyboard‑accessible forms on Order Online—tab order is logical, focus styles are visible.

Mobile Design Concepts

  • Responsive grid via CSS Grid & media queries: 4‑column on desktop, 2‑column on tablet, 1‑column on phone.
  • Touch‑friendly tap targets (≥44 px) on all buttons and links.
  • <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1"> enabled for proper scaling.
