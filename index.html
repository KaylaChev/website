Look and Feel of the Awaken Delights Website

Page Layout & Grid System: The site follows a clean, mobile-responsive layout. Key sections include a sticky header with navigation, a bold hero statement, a flexible services grid, and a simple footer. The layout uses CSS Flexbox for adaptability and visual balance.

Typography: The site uses the Arial sans-serif font for a clean, modern feel. Headings are bold and sized for hierarchy, while body text is easy to read with sufficient line height. Font sizes adjust for readability on all screen sizes.

Color Scheme: A calming, pastel-inspired palette supports the cozy and inviting vibe of a neighborhood bakery:

  • Primary: Sage Green (#9BC1BC)
  • Accent: Warm Coral (#F49097)
  • Background: Soft Cream (#F4F1BB)
  • Text: Dark Gray (#333333)

Imagery Style: Whimsical and authentic photos of real bakery staff, customers, and baked goods will reflect the community-oriented mission. Product images are bright, cozy, and often feature natural textures like wood and linen for warmth.

Logo: The logo features a modern, whimsical design with a sunrise rising behind a loaf of bread, symbolizing a fresh start. It incorporates site colors and uses smooth, curved lines to communicate warmth and joy. (See image for reference.)

Interactive Page Widgets:

  • Responsive navigation bar with hover highlights
  • Collapsible menus for mobile users
  • A contact form with input fields for user messages
  • Email signup form in the footer for newsletter growth
  • Hover states on product cards and buttons to encourage interaction
  • Alt text on all images for accessibility
