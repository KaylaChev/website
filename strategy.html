Site Strategy

How we’ll become the “beloved community bakery” and reach 100 online orders/month:

1. Authentic Brand Storytelling

  • Feature behind‑the‑scenes baker profiles on About Us.
  • Show ingredient sourcing and daily process on homepage.

2. Seamless eCommerce Experience

  • “Add to Cart” CTAs on every product card.
  • Guest checkout + Apple/Google Pay

3. Local SEO & Content Marketing

  • Optimize for “Anytown bakery” and publish weekly blog posts.

4. Community Engagement & Retention

  • Bread Club loyalty program: 10th loaf free.
  • Bi‑weekly newsletter with subscriber‑only discounts.

5. Data‑Driven Improvement

  • Track conversion, A/B test homepage hero.
