✅ Specific Tactics

  • Post a “Weekly Special” banner on the homepage
    → Encourages repeat visits by promoting something new each week.
  • Offer an email signup form with a 10% discount on first order
    → Builds a customer list while giving value upfront.
  • Add high-quality images of each menu item with fun descriptions
    → Helps users visualize and crave the product, encouraging purchases.
  • Include a “Behind the Scenes” section on the About page
    → Builds emotional connection by showing the people and process behind the bakery.

🚫 Anti-Tactics to Avoid

  • Avoid autoplaying music or videos
    → Can annoy users, especially mobile visitors, and slow down the site.
  • Avoid cluttered navigation with too many menu items
    → Overwhelms visitors and makes it harder to find key content.
